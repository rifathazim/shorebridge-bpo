A dedicated outsourced call center agent costs **$6–9 per hour in Bangladesh**, **$8–12 per hour in the Philippines**, and **$25–35 per hour for a US in-house hire** once you include salary, benefits, software, and management overhead. [PLACEHOLDER: verify figures before launch]

Here is how those numbers break down, and what vendors usually leave out of the quote.

## The real cost comparison

| Cost item | US in-house | Philippines BPO | Bangladesh BPO |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hourly rate (dedicated agent) | $18–25 wage | $8–12 billed | $6–9 billed |
| Benefits & payroll taxes | +25–35% | included | included |
| Recruiting & HR | $3,000–5,000/hire | included | included |
| Workstation, software, telephony | $200–400/mo | included | included |
| Supervision | you | included | included |
| **Fully loaded hourly** | **$25–35** | **$8–12** | **$6–9** |

The BPO rates are all-inclusive: the billed hourly rate covers wage, facilities, equipment, telephony, quality monitoring, and a team lead.

## What drives the rate inside the range

- **Language tier.** Agents handling complex voice support in fluent English sit at the top of the range; chat and email support sits lower.
- **Shift.** US business hours are night shift in Dhaka — that carries a modest premium over daytime work.
- **Team size.** One agent pays list rate. Five or more seats typically earns a volume discount.

## The hidden costs to ask any vendor about

1. **Setup and training fees.** Some vendors charge $500–2,000 per seat to start. Ask up front. (ShoreBridge doesn't. [PLACEHOLDER: confirm terms])
2. **Minimum commitments.** Twelve-month lock-ins are common in the industry; month-to-month should be the default you push for.
3. **Attrition and retraining.** If the vendor churns agents, you pay in quality. Ask what percentage of agents stay past one year.
4. **Management layers.** A quote that looks cheap sometimes hides a thin supervision layer — meaning you end up managing agents yourself.

## Bottom line

For a single dedicated agent working US hours, expect roughly **$1,000–1,500 per month in Bangladesh** versus **$4,300–6,000 in-house in the US**. For most support workloads the quality difference, honestly managed, is small — and the savings are not.
