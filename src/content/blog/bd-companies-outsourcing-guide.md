Outsourcing isn't only for foreign companies. **A growing number of Bangladeshi businesses — banks, manufacturers, hospitals, trading houses — now hand their data entry, customer support, and design work to local BPO teams in Dhaka** instead of hiring, training, and managing that staff in-house.

Here's how it works, what it costs, and what to hand off first.

## Why outsource locally instead of hiring?

- **Speed.** A BPO team is productive in weeks. A new in-house department takes months of recruiting, HR setup, and management attention.
- **Flexibility.** Scale a five-person data team down to two after your project ends — try doing that with permanent hires.
- **Focus.** Your managers manage your business, not an internal call center.
- **Cost is comparable, capability is not.** You pay near in-house rates but get trained staff, QA processes, supervision, and coverage for leave/attrition built in.

## What Bangladeshi companies hand off first

1. **Customer support lines** — inbound calls, complaint desks, order status.
2. **Data entry and digitization** — paper records, KYC forms, survey data, ERP migration.
3. **Design and social media** — steady content output without a full creative department.
4. **Outbound follow-up** — payment reminders, delivery confirmations, feedback calls.

## How to start without risk

- **Start with one process, not a department.** Pick a well-defined, measurable task — say, digitizing one backlog of records — and judge the output.
- **Insist on a trial month.** Any serious local BPO will agree.
- **Keep your data in your systems.** Good vendors work inside accounts you control, so nothing leaves your infrastructure.
- **Meet the actual team.** In Dhaka this is easy — visit the floor, meet the people doing your work. If a vendor won't show you, walk away.
